Apotheosis Enchanting

5 new stats replace
flat bookshelf levels
EternaThe main stat — each point raises the enchanting level cap by 2. It's what lets you go past vanilla's level 30 ceiling.
QuantaHow much your result can swing above or below what you spent. High Quanta means less consistent enchants — for better or worse.
ArcanaShifts the rarity odds toward the good stuff, and guarantees extra enchantments — 2 minimum at 25%, 3 at 75%.
RectificationRaises the low end of that Quanta swing, so a bad roll is less bad. Negative Rectification does the opposite.
CluesHow many of the available enchantments you're actually shown before committing to enchant.
Enchanting & anvil costs are optimal
not a flat charge off your current levels

Values are per shelf block placed. Stone, melon, and bee shelves are early, cheap, and trade Eterna away for something else — bee shelves in particular tank your level cap for a huge Quanta swing. Sea and hell shelves are a straight step up; deep and end shelves are the late-game tier, strong across the board with a much higher Eterna cap.
