Leveling System

Every player levels up, and the world gets tougher the farther you wander from spawn.

Infernal Mobs

Almost any mob can roll Elite, Ultra, or Infernal
on spawn, stacking random combat abilities as it climbs
Elite~1 in 10 · 1 ability
Ultra~1 in 140 · 2 abilities
Infernal~1 in 2,800 · 3 abilities
Each tier rolls on top of the last, from a shared pool of ~20 abilities: lifesteal, regeneration, berserking, wither, fire, gravity wells, a second life, and more. Actual bosses and minibosses never roll this; it's strictly for the mobs already covered under World Scaling.
Better tier, better drop
Gem Dust and Rarity Material scale with it, up to Sigils and Ancient Material on an Infernal kill

Transformations

Ten Legendaries have no recipe and never drop
you make them from another Legendary
These won't show up in JEI: there's no recipe to display, and they're excluded from the chest-loot pool. Every one starts from a Legendary you found normally.
→ The DevourerReforge a Watcher Claymore in the Deep Dark ▶

The Devourer is an alternate form of The Watcher, and the gateway to four more weapons below.

  1. Find a Watcher Claymore: it's in the normal chest-loot pool.
  2. Awaken it to level 4 or higher at a Runic Forge.
  3. Reforge it at a Runic Forge located in the Deep Dark.

Reforging it again below level 4 turns it back into The Watcher, so the change is reversible.

→ GloampiercerFeed a Wickpiercer to The Devourer's mass ▶
  1. Get The Devourer first (above).
  2. Right-click with it to launch an abyssal seed and grow a Devouring Mass.
  3. Drop a Wickpiercer into the mass: it consumes the weapon and corrupts it.

Wickpiercer is ordinary chest loot. Gloampiercer is a spear: 10 damage, 1.9 speed, 15–35% chance to ignore armour.

→ DreadwhisperFeed a Whisperwind to The Devourer's mass ▶

Same method as Gloampiercer: grow a Devouring Mass with The Devourer, then drop a Whisperwind into it.

Unique effect: Shadow Rend. Fully awakened it reaches 9 damage.

→ SoulstalkerFeed a Soulrender to The Devourer's mass ▶

Same method again: grow a Devouring Mass, then drop a Soulrender into it.

Unique effect: Stygian Stride. Fully awakened it reaches 9 damage.

→ WraithmawFeed a Wraithfang to The Devourer's mass ▶

Same method again: grow a Devouring Mass, then drop a Wraithfang into it.

Unique effect: Spectral Downpour. Fully awakened it reaches 7 damage.

→ Sunfire & HarbingerAwaken a Dormant Relic: the time of day picks the route ▶

The Dormant Relic is normal chest loot. Which weapon it ends up as is decided by when you hit level 4.

  1. Awaken the Dormant Relic to level 4 at a Runic Forge.
  2. It splits by time of day, in the Overworld.
  3. Keep awakening that relic to level 8 for the final form.
Daytime → Righteous Relic (chance on hit to regenerate) → at level 8 becomes Sunfire
Night → Tainted Relic (chance on hit to inflict weakness) → at level 8 becomes Harbinger
→ Magiscythe, Magiblade & MagispearCarry a Decaying Relic into the Deep Dark ▶

The Decaying Relic reacts to the Deep Dark. Keep it in your inventory while exploring the deepest, darkest parts of the Overworld: it keeps its components and awakening level when it changes form, so nothing is lost.

Take a Warden sonic boom → becomes Magiscythe
Stand on a Sculk Sensor → chance to become Magiblade
Experience Darkness → rarer chance to become Magispear
→ Waking & Awakened LichbladeJust keep adding tablets: it changes shape as it levels ▶

The Lichblade changes form in the Runic Forge as tablets go in. No special location or condition: only the awakening level matters.

Level 0–3: Slumbering Lichblade, Soul Anguish I (this is the one you find in chests)
Level 4–7: Waking Lichblade, Soul Anguish II
Level 8: Awakened Lichblade, Soul Anguish III, unlocks the returning-soul absorption
→ Ionbound StormscaleReforge a Stormscale during a thunderstorm ▶
  1. Find a Stormscale: normal chest loot.
  2. Awaken it to level 4 or higher.
  3. Reforge it at a Runic Forge while a thunderstorm is happening.

Apotheosis Enchanting

A deeper enchanting system: 5 new stats replace flat bookshelf levels.

5 new stats replace
flat bookshelf levels
EternaThe main stat: each point raises the enchanting level cap by 2. It's what lets you go past vanilla's level 30 ceiling.
QuantaHow much your result can swing above or below what you spent. High Quanta means less consistent enchants, for better or worse.
ArcanaShifts the rarity odds toward the good stuff, and guarantees extra enchantments: 2 minimum at 25%, 3 at 75%.
RectificationRaises the low end of that Quanta swing, so a bad roll is less bad. Negative Rectification does the opposite.
CluesHow many of the available enchantments you're actually shown before committing to enchant.
Enchanting & anvil costs are optimal
not a flat charge off your current levels

Affix Types

Stat AffixesA flat bonus to a single attribute. The most common affix type, and the only one that can appear on a Common item.
Effect AffixesBigger, weirder bonuses: utility or straight combat power. Rare and above only.
SocketsA slot that holds one Gem. Uncommon and above only, and never more than the rarity's cap.
DurabilityMakes the item more resistant to breaking. Guaranteed on Rare and above, scaling with rarity.

Gems

Socketable Gems
Each gem gives a different bonus depending on the item it's socketed into, and some are Unique: only one copy of that gem can be socketed at a time. Socket one by combining gem + item in a smithing table.
VIAL OF SEARING EXPULSION
Keeps the item
destroys the gems
VIAL OF ARCANE EXTRACTION
Keeps the gems
destroys the item
Combine either vial with a socketed item in a smithing table to pull the gems back out, then pick whichever half of the pair you actually want to keep.

Reforging

Reforging rerolls an item's affixes entirely; it works on any valid item, not just ones that already rolled affixes.

Up to Rare rarity
→
REFORGING TABLE
Epic rarity and above
Cheap reroll2 each
Mid reroll4 each
Strong reroll6 each
"Each" = Gem Dust, Rarity Material, and XP levels. Fill all three slots (item, Rarity Material, Gem Dust) and pick one of three rerolled outcomes; no choice is inherently stronger, they're just different rolls. No limit on how many times an item can be reforged.
